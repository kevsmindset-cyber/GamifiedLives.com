Habitify vs. Gamified Lives: The Data-Rich Tracker vs. the Habit RPG

Nov 9, 2026 · 9 min read · by Kevin Castaneda

Habitify is what a lot of people mean by "a serious habit tracker": clean, fast, data-rich, and quietly excellent at pulling in steps, workouts and sleep from your watch so you don't have to log anything. Gamified Lives turns the same raw material into a game with a hero, a weekly boss and a coach. Different jobs, overlapping buyers.

I build Gamified Lives. I also think Habitify is one of the two or three best pure trackers on iOS, so this is a comparison, not a takedown.

Where Habitify wins

Automatic tracking is the headline. Connect your watch and your workouts, steps and sleep log themselves, which removes the single biggest reason trackers die: having to open the app. The week view, the calendar heatmaps and the per-habit analytics are the best in the category. It runs everywhere, including a real Mac app and Apple Watch complications. And the lifetime price exists, which many people prefer to another subscription.

If you want a precise, fast, honest record of what you did, Habitify is the pick, and it's not close.

Where it loses people

Habitify is built around the streak. It's a well-designed streak, with a calendar that makes a miss obvious and a "habit strength" score that softens the blow, but it is still a system where the proudest number can drop to zero on a Tuesday. There's nothing pulling you back after a bad week except the app's own reminder, which you've learned to swipe away by week three.

The second gap is that a record isn't a reason. Habitify shows you the data beautifully, but the motivation has to come from you. For people who already have it, that's fine. For people who downloaded a habit app because they don't, it's the whole problem.

Where Gamified Lives fits

We add the reason. Each week you pick a boss for the part of life you're pushing, and every real task you finish hits it. Tasks in that area hit twice as hard. A task you prove with a photo or Apple Health crits. Skip a day and the boss heals a little; nothing is ever taken from you. Beat it and you get coins and rare gear for a hero you built yourself.

Underneath the game is a scoring system that rewards the comeback instead of punishing the miss: time invested on the home screen, double XP for returning within 24 hours, and a deliberate absence of a visible streak. And instead of plugging into a general chatbot, the coach is built in: it knows your goals and the week you just had, writes a weekly report across all six life areas, and calls you when you go quiet.

Where we lose. Habitify's integrations are broader. We read Apple Health, but we don't pull Strava or Fitbit, and there's no Mac app, no Android, no Apple Watch yet. Our analytics are good but not Habitify's. And there's no lifetime option.

How to choose

  1. Do you need the data, or the push? If you'll do the habits anyway and want a perfect record, Habitify. If the doing is the problem, Gamified Lives.
  2. Is your tracking mostly automatic? Steps, runs and sleep from a watch favor Habitify. Habits a sensor can't see favor proof-based completion.
  3. What does a missed week do to you? If a zeroed streak sends you away, pick the system where nothing resets.

Frequently asked questions

Is Habitify free?

Yes, with limits. Habitify Pro is $8.99 a month, $49.99 a year, or $119.99 for lifetime access, with a family plan at $89.99 a year. Gamified Lives is free for habits, goals, the hero and the weekly boss; Premium is $9.99 a month or $75.99 a year.

Habitify vs Gamified Lives: which is better?

Different jobs. Habitify is the better pure tracker: automatic tracking from Apple Health, Fitbit and Strava, strong analytics, and apps on every platform. Gamified Lives is better when motivation is the problem: a weekly boss your real tasks damage, a hero you build, and a coach that calls, with a system that never resets on a miss.

Does Habitify have streaks?

Yes. Streaks and a habit strength score are central to Habitify, with a calendar that shows every gap. Gamified Lives hides the streak and shows time invested instead, and pays double XP for coming back within 24 hours of a miss.

Does Gamified Lives work with Apple Health?

Yes. Steps, workouts, mindful minutes and sleep from Apple Health auto-verify matching habits, and verified completions deal extra damage to the weekly boss.
