Fabulous vs. Gamified Lives: Guided Routines or a Game You Can't Lose?

Nov 9, 2026 · 9 min read · by Kevin Castaneda

Fabulous is the app most people think of when they think "build a morning routine." It's been around for a decade, it has tens of thousands of ratings, and its guided programs are genuinely good. Gamified Lives is a different animal: a self-improvement RPG where your tasks hit a weekly boss and the whole system is built for the day after a miss.

I build Gamified Lives, so read this knowing that. I've also used Fabulous on and off since 2019, and I'll tell you plainly where it's the better pick.

Where Fabulous wins

If you don't know what your morning should look like, Fabulous will tell you, one small step at a time. Drink water. Then a short stretch. Then a real breakfast. The "journeys" are paced carefully, the audio is warm, and the content library on anxiety, focus and sleep is deeper than anything a one-person app can match. For someone who wants to be led, it's the best-produced product in the category.

It also has the longest track record. Ten years of iteration shows in the small things: the onboarding, the letter-style reminders, the animations.

Where it loses people

Fabulous is a program you follow, and programs assume you're following. When life interrupts, the app's answer is to restart the journey, and restarting something you were two weeks into feels like losing those two weeks. That is the quiet reason people drift off around week four. There's no scoring that survives a bad stretch, and no mechanism that rewards the return specifically.

The second issue is that it's a guided experience rather than a tracker. If you already know your habits and want to see your own data, the structure gets in the way. And the pricing is confusing: the App Store lists Premium at half a dozen different prices, and the trial-to-paid flow has drawn the kind of complaints that come with aggressive renewals.

Where Gamified Lives fits

We started from the opposite end. Instead of a program to follow, you set goals, add habits, and the app turns them into daily tasks. Then it makes those tasks matter in two ways Fabulous doesn't.

First, the weekly boss. You pick the part of life you're pushing this week, and every task you finish hits it. Tasks in that area hit twice as hard. A task you prove with a photo crits. Skip a day and the boss heals a little, which is the only penalty in the whole app. Second, the comeback: your home screen shows time invested, a number that only goes up, and coming back within 24 hours of a miss pays double XP. I've written about why we killed the streak entirely.

The coach is also a different kind of thing. Fabulous gives you a recorded voice. Ours is an AI that knows your goals, your habits and the week you just had, and it calls you when you go quiet, as a real voice call. That's the paid part, along with the weekly report and rare gear.

Where we lose. We don't have a content library. If you want someone to design your routine for you, Fabulous does that and we don't. We're iOS only and far younger, so expect rough edges Fabulous sanded off years ago. And our Premium is more per month, though the free tier covers everything in the loop.

How to choose

  1. Do you want to be led, or to run your own list? Led: Fabulous. Your own list with a game on top: Gamified Lives.
  2. How do you handle a bad week? If you quit apps after the first one, pick the app whose rule for that week you can live with. Ours is "nothing resets."
  3. Does a tapped checkbox count for you? If you know you'll tap "done" on things you didn't do, you need proof-based completion, and only one of the two has it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fabulous worth paying for?

If you want a guided routine with audio coaching and a deep content library, yes. Fabulous Premium runs about $49.99 a year, with offers from $16.99 to $59.99 depending on the plan. If you already know your habits and want your own data, you'll be paying for content you won't use.

What is a good alternative to Fabulous?

It depends on what's missing. For a game layer and a forgiving system that rewards coming back after a miss, Gamified Lives. For a data-heavy tracker with integrations, Habitify. For a community and quests, Habitica.

Does Fabulous punish you for missing a day?

Not with a penalty, but your journey stalls and the app nudges you to restart it, which is where many people drop off. Gamified Lives takes nothing away on a miss and pays double XP for coming back within 24 hours.

Is Gamified Lives free?

Yes. Habits, goals, all six life areas, the hero and the weekly boss are free. Premium at $9.99 a month or $75.99 a year adds the AI coach with voice calls, the weekly report, and rare gear, with a 7-day trial.
