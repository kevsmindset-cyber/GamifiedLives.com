Fair questions.

Is it actually free?

Yes. All core tasks, habits and goals, all six domains, tracking, the Phoenix Bonus, common cosmetics, and squads are free — no card. Premium ($9.99/month or $75.99/year, 7-day free trial) adds the AI coach chat and calls, the weekly report, every domain's AI tool, and rare cosmetics.

Is it iOS only?

Today, yes — it's on the App Store now. There's no Android or web version yet, so please don't buy a phone for us. Leave your email above and you'll hear the moment that changes.

Is this another shame-streak app?

The opposite. Your home screen shows Time Played — a number that only goes up. Miss a day and nothing is taken away; come back within 24 hours and the Phoenix Bonus pays 2× XP. The whole system is built for the day after a miss.

What makes it different from Habitica or Finch?

Habitica punishes the miss (HP damage); gentle pet apps let you quietly fade out. Gamified Lives rewards the comeback and actively pulls you back — including an AI coach that gives you a real voice call when you go quiet. And it covers all six life areas, not one.

Do I need to be into games?

No. The RPG layer is a reason to open the app, not homework. If you never touch a cosmetic, it's still a life tracker with a forgiving scoring system and a coach. If you do like games — you'll feel at home.
