Boilerplate

Copy-paste description

Gamified Lives is a self-improvement RPG for iOS that turns real habits into quests and real progress into XP. Unlike streak-based apps, it's forgiveness-first: missing a day costs nothing, and coming back within 24 hours earns a 2× Phoenix Bonus. It tracks six life domains — Health, Sleep, Wealth, Mind, Growth, and Recovery — each gamified into a skill you level, and includes an AI coach that checks in with a real voice call when you go quiet. Built by solo founder Kevin Castaneda. Free on the App Store; Premium is $9.99/month or $75.99/year.
