About

Built by someone who quit
every habit app he tried.

Gamified Lives is made by one person. Self-funded. No VC, no growth team, no roadmap someone else wrote.

Hi — I’m Kevin Castaneda. I’ve been obsessed with habit apps for years, and I abandoned every single one the same way: not on a hard day, but the morning after. Miss once, and the streak I was proud of hit zero. Opening the app felt like walking into a lecture, so I stopped opening it.

That morning-after moment is where every habit app loses you. So it’s the moment I built Gamified Lives around.

The belief
The comeback matters more than the streak. Never-broken is a myth. Rebuilt is the skill.

Why forgiveness-first

Most habit apps run on loss aversion: keep the number alive or lose everything. It works right up until your first bad day — then it becomes the reason you quit. Gamified Lives flips it. Miss a day and nothing is taken from you. Come back within 24 hours and the Phoenix Bonus pays 2× XP. Your headline number is Time Played — it only goes up. Your Resilience Score measures how well you return, not how perfectly you show up.

This isn’t softness for its own sake — the research on self-compassion and habit formation backs it, and so does everyone who has ever deleted an app out of shame.

Why one app for your whole life

The day I realized I had six apps for six areas of my life — macros, sleep, money, mood, learning, sobriety — was the day the design changed. Gamified Lives absorbs them into six domains: Health, Sleep, Wealth, Mind, Growth, Recovery. You pick which ones you want; the rest stay hidden.

The magic is what happens when the data lives together. The app finds correlations for you: “You spend 30% more on days after shorter sleep.” No single-purpose tracker can see that, because no single-purpose tracker has the full picture.

Why an RPG

If a game can make you sit through a 60-hour side quest on progress bars and vibes alone, a habit app should be able to make you save $5,000, sleep 7.5 hours, or read 20 pages a night. Goals become quests, real progress becomes XP, and you — an AI-generated avatar of actual you — are the character who levels up.

What’s next

Gamified Lives is live on iOS today. Beyond that, the plan is whatever the community says it is — the subreddit is the real planning doc, and the roadmap is public.
